## 1. Summary

**What FlexiPath is.** [Verified: README, CLAUDE.md]

- FlexiPath is a **simulation prototype**. It is not a working network product.
- It models a predictive, traffic-aware connectivity engine for a drone that flies through Wi-Fi, 5G and LEO satellite coverage.
- The engine watches each link's trend and predicts a failure. It wakes and tests the next network, briefly sends critical traffic over both, and switches before the old link breaks.
- A UDP relay keeps one address for all sessions.
- It is compared with four other stacks:
  - a legacy break-before-make stack;
  - "reactive multipath", modelled on an MPTCP backup subflow or ATSSS active-standby;
  - "always duplicate", modelled on the MPTCP redundant scheduler or PDCP duplication;
  - three FlexiPath variants, each with one part switched off (ablations).

### 8.2 Technical limits judges could question

[Verified: README limits section, code]

- **Simulation only.** It uses a stylised RF model, a ground-level radio model for a flying drone, and an assumed power model. There are no real-world measurements.
- **The key "kept C2" result in D, G and H depends on an assumption.** FlexiPath detects an abrupt loss in 60 ms, the reactive baseline in 250 ms. With equal detection, the reactive baseline also keeps C2 there.
- **The 300 ms hover rule is our own choice.**
  - It is applied at all speeds.
  - Under the 3GPP waypoint rule (1 s), every multipath stack keeps C2.
  - Under a speed-consistent rule, always duplicate keeps C2 in 18 of 20 flights in G.
- **Modem wake of 100 ms is optimistic.** At 260 ms, FlexiPath also hovers in D and H.
- **The 400 ms duplication burst shows no reliability gain** in the model.
- **Only FlexiPath gets the coverage map.**
- Traffic classes do not share capacity, so E = A.
- **FlexiPath gives up throughput in G and H.** It delivers 71.9 vs 82.4 Mbit/s in G, and in H it is even below legacy.
- **The legacy stack's TCP/TLS stall also blocks UDP.** A UDP-only C2 gap would be about 2.3 s instead of 3.8 s.
- **No tests, linter or type checker.** Correctness rests on the deterministic benchmark.
- **Source maps are on in `vite.config.js`.** Deploys turn them off by hand; a deploy that forgets would publish the source of a private repo.
- **The Vercel deploy is manual**, from one laptop and one account.
- Several citations lack page, section or table numbers (section 3.8).
